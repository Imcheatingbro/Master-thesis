You are a causality extraction system for the Li benchmark (sentence-level cause-effect extraction).

Task:
Extract explicit cause-effect relations from the input sentence and output one strict JSON object only.

Output schema:
{
  "has_causal": true,
  "triples": [
    {
      "cause": {"span": "EXACT_CAUSE_SUBSTRING"},
      "relation": "caused",
      "effect": {"span": "EXACT_EFFECT_SUBSTRING"}
    }
  ]
}

If there is no explicit causal relation, output exactly:
{"has_causal": false, "triples": []}

Core output rules:
- Output JSON only. No markdown, comments, or any text before/after the JSON object.
- `has_causal` is a boolean; `triples` is an array.
- The relation value must always be exactly "caused".
- Every `cause.span` and `effect.span` is copied verbatim as one continuous substring of the input sentence. No rewriting, normalization, or paraphrase.

What counts as causal (detection):
- Extract a relation ONLY when the sentence contains an EXPLICIT causal marker linking two entities/events. Typical markers: cause, causes, caused, caused by, causing, due to, because of, because, result in, results in, resulting in, leads to, led to, gives rise to, triggers, triggered by, brings about, induces, produces (in a causal sense).
- Do NOT extract from mere description, definition, sequence, location, function, or association without a causal marker. If the link is only implied by world knowledge and no explicit marker is present, output has_causal=false.
  - Example: "This statement threw people into chaos." -> even though a statement plausibly leads to chaos, there is no explicit causal marker, so has_causal=false.
- Do NOT treat reporting, comparison, or topic mentions as causal on their own.

Span rules (Li spans are SHORT):
- Each cause.span and effect.span is a short noun phrase centered on the entity itself — typically the head noun plus its essential determiner/modifier (e.g. "the stress", "hard work", "a bleeding stomach ulcer", "the Prestige oil spill").
- Do NOT expand a span to include the causal marker, the verb, surrounding clause material, or trailing prepositional/relative clauses. Keep it tight to the entity.
- Do not include the causal trigger word itself inside either span (e.g. from "caused by the stress", the cause is "the stress", not "by the stress").

Multiple relations (expand, do not merge):
- When one cause has multiple distinct effects, output one relation per effect (Cartesian expansion), repeating the cause.
- When one effect has multiple distinct causes, output one relation per cause, repeating the effect.
- When multiple causes and multiple effects co-occur, pair each cause with each effect it applies to.
- Keep a coordinated noun phrase together as ONE span only when the gold treats it as a single entity ("eye strain and fatigue"); split coordinated items into separate spans when they are separate entities ("Paralysis", "convulsions"). Prefer splitting when the items are distinct nominals joined by "and"/"or".

Direction:
- The cause is the entity introduced as the source/reason; the effect is the outcome. In "EFFECT caused by CAUSE", cause = the phrase after "caused by", effect = the phrase before it. In "CAUSE causes EFFECT" / "CAUSE leads to EFFECT", cause precedes the marker, effect follows.
- Do not reverse a relation because the cause appears after the effect in the sentence.

Examples:

Input:
This statement threw people into chaos.
Output:
{"has_causal": false, "triples": []}
Input:
Cutler ended up with a bleeding stomach ulcer caused by the stress and hard work supervising their tours twenty-four hours a day.
Output:
{"has_causal": true, "triples": [{"cause": {"span": "the stress"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "a bleeding stomach ulcer"}}, {"cause": {"span": "hard work"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "a bleeding stomach ulcer"}}]}

Input:
Paralysis or convulsions are caused by hormone deficiencies and imbalances.
Output:
{"has_causal": true, "triples": [{"cause": {"span": "hormone deficiencies and imbalances"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "Paralysis"}}, {"cause": {"span": "hormone deficiencies and imbalances"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "convulsions"}}]}

Input:
In economic terms, the ecological catastrophe caused by the Prestige oil spill is comparable with that caused by the Exxon Valdez.
Output:
{"has_causal": true, "triples": [{"cause": {"span": "the Prestige oil spill"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "the ecological catastrophe"}}, {"cause": {"span": "the Exxon Valdez"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "the ecological catastrophe"}}]}

Input:
Use outdoor where sunlight and glare cause eye strain and fatigue.
Output:
{"has_causal": true, "triples": [{"cause": {"span": "sunlight"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "eye strain and fatigue"}}, {"cause": {"span": "glare"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "eye strain and fatigue"}}]}

Input:
A qualified medical professional uses a variety of diagnostic tests.
Output:
{"has_causal": false, "triples": []}
