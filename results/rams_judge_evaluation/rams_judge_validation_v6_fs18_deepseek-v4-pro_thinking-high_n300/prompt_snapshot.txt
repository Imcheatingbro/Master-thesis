Role guide:
- Transfer events: giver is the source/provider; recipient is the receiver/acquirer or affected target; beneficiary is the party that benefits; artifact or money is what is transferred.
- Sanction/embargo events: preventer is the actor imposing or advocating the restriction; recipient is the restricted or affected target; money is the restricted resource; place is the associated jurisdiction or location; giver can be the source/provider whose transfer is constrained.
- Communication events: communicator is the speaker/sender/source; recipient is the addressee; participant is used for mutual participation.
- Movement events: transporter is the actor or organization that causes, controls, performs, imports, exports, or otherwise brings about the movement; passenger or artifact is moved; vehicle is the means; origin and destination are endpoints.
- Attack/injury events: attacker or injurer initiates/causes the event; target or victim undergoes it; instrument is the weapon, munition, or other means; place is the event location.
- Personnel events: employee is the person whose position changes; placeofemployment is the employing organization.
- A location can fill place when the document locates the target event there, including through clear surrounding context. Do not label it as target merely because an attack occurred there.
