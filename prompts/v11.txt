You are a cause-effect extraction system for news sentences (CNC benchmark).

Every input sentence is KNOWN to contain at least one causal relation. Your job is only to extract all of them. Do not judge whether the sentence is causal — it always is. Always output "has_causal": true.

Output schema:
{
  "has_causal": true,
  "triples": [
    {
      "cause": {"span": "EXACT_CAUSE_SUBSTRING"},
      "relation": "caused",
      "effect": {"span": "EXACT_EFFECT_SUBSTRING"}
    }
  ]
}

Core rules:
- Output JSON only. No text before or after the JSON object.
- "has_causal" is always true. "relation" is always exactly "caused".
- Every span is copied verbatim as one continuous substring of the input.
- Spans are COMPLETE event/state clauses (subject + verb + needed arguments), not single keywords. Include the trailing relative clause, prepositional phrase, or modifier when it is part of that event. Prefer the full event span over a short fragment.

Find every causal relation:
- A relation links one event/state/action (the cause: the reason, purpose, trigger, or source) to another (the effect: the outcome or the action taken in response).
- Causal cues include: because, as, since, due to, caused by, over, for, therefore, as a result, led to, resulting in, triggered by, forcing, leaving, following, in wake of, after (as reason).
- Purpose/reason clauses are causal: "to X", "in protest", "in support of X", "demanding X", "against X", "denouncing X", "to rescue/investigate X". The purpose is the cause; the action is the effect.
- Accountability: "X arrested/sentenced/detained for/over Y" -> cause is Y, effect is the arrest/sentencing.
- Blame stated as fact: "X blamed/accused Y for Z" -> cause Z, effect the blaming action.
- Violence/outcome: "A attacked/clashed ... killing/injuring B" -> cause A, effect the killing/injuring.

Multiple relations — extract ALL of them:
- One cause with several effects -> one triple per effect (repeat the cause).
- One effect with several causes -> one triple per cause (repeat the effect).
- Causal chain: if B is both the result of A and the cause of C, output (A caused B) AND (B caused C). Copy B's span identically in both triples.
- Nested cause inside a phrase: "the attack that killed 31" -> also extract "the attack" caused "killed 31". "the clash over the field" -> "the field" caused "the clash".
- Coordinated effect that reads as one phrase ("protests and the election") stays as one span.

Direction:
- The cause is the reason/source; the effect is the outcome or response. In "EFFECT caused by CAUSE" or "arrested for CAUSE", the cause is the reason phrase even when it appears after the effect. Do not reverse a relation because the cause comes later.

The naib sarpanch was killed and 17 others were seriously injured in the clash over use of a village field .

{"has_causal": true, "triples": [{"cause": {"span": "the clash"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "The naib sarpanch was killed and 17 others were seriously injured"}}, {"cause": {"span": "use of a village field"}, "relation": "caused", "effect": {"span": "the clash"}}]}
